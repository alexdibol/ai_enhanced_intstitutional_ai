The institutional pipeline

Governed knowledge, not an unrestricted model connection

The central proposition is simple but consequential: the strategic unit of organizational AI is not the model alone. It is the complete institutional system that joins human judgment, protected knowledge, generative and reasoning models, and governed institutional memory. A model should not receive unrestricted access to a vault; it should receive the minimum eligible evidence required for a declared purpose, through an explicit and auditable context-construction process.

Main paper

AI-Enhanced Institutional Knowledge

This synthesis paper is the intellectual spine of the repository. It follows institutional knowledge from protected source vaults into canonical objects and typed relationships, then through governed model access, candidate generation, authorization, recursive write-back, evolving cognitive topology, evidence selection, deliberation, benchmarking, and dependency-aware reversal. Its central concern is not merely what AI can infer, but how an institution can allow AI to learn without silently converting generation into authority.
